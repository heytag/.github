# heytag

**Server-side tagging — hosted and operated in Germany.**

heytag provides managed hosting and operations for server-side Google Tag Manager,
built for companies that take data privacy seriously.

## What we do

- **Server-side GTM hosting** — your tagging infrastructure runs on our servers,
  not in the visitor's browser
- **100% Germany** — servers, company, and operations are exclusively based in Germany
- **Privacy by design** — built to help you meet GDPR requirements without
  compromising on analytics and marketing capabilities

## Why server-side tagging?

Server-side tagging moves tracking logic from the browser to a controlled server
environment. This means better data quality, faster page loads, and full control
over what data leaves your infrastructure.
